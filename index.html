How it works

Three steps, about a minute. You export from WhatsApp, drop the file here, and Threadline does the rest on your device.

Export from WhatsAppUse WhatsApp’s built-in “Export chat”. Choose “Include media” to unlock the photo, video and file vault.
Drop it in hereDrop the .zip as-is. No need to unzip it. You will be asked once which side of the chat is you.
ExploreSeven views: Overview, Timeline, Calendar, Media, Links, Chat and Wrapped. A short tour shows you around.

How to export a chat

Do this on your phone, for one chat at a time. WhatsApp on the desktop can’t export.

  1. Open the chat, then tap ⋮ (top right) → More → Export chat.
  2. Choose Include media for the full experience, or Without media for a much smaller, text-only file.
  3. Send the file to yourself (Drive, Gmail, Telegram, USB…) and open it on the device where you’re using Threadline. Or open Threadline on your phone and pick the file straight from Files.
  1. Open the chat, then tap the contact or group name at the top.
  2. Scroll down and tap Export Chat, then choose Attach Media or Without Media.
  3. In the share sheet choose Save to Files, AirDrop or Mail. Then drop that .zip here, or pick it from Files on this phone.
WhatsApp caps exports: roughly the latest 10,000 messages with media, or 40,000 without. For a long chat, a text-only export still covers far more history.

Your chat stays yours

Never uploadedYour file is read and analysed inside this browser tab. The app makes no network requests, so there is nowhere for it to go.
Saved on this device onlySo you can reopen a chat without importing again, it is kept in your browser’s own storage. Nobody else can see it.
You are in control“Forget” removes a chat, its media, tags and notes from this browser. The original export on your phone is never touched.

Common questions

Do I need to unzip the export?
No. Drop the .zip directly. If you already unzipped it, drop the .txt file together with all the media files at once.
Which format and language does it understand?
Android and iPhone exports, 12 or 24-hour clocks, and day-first, month-first or year-first dates. The date order is detected automatically from your chat.
Why does the Media view say “Not exported”?

You exported “Without media”, or WhatsApp skipped older files because of its size cap. Threadline can still see that a photo or video was sent, but has no file to show.

Re-export with media to fill the vault.

How big a chat can it handle?
Parsing runs in the background so the page stays responsive, and media files are read on demand instead of being loaded into memory all at once, so large exports are designed to work. Very large chats take a few seconds to open, and a text-only export is much faster.
Can I analyse a group chat?
Yes. Groups get a leaderboard (sortable by messages, words, reply speed and more) and a “who replies to whom” matrix instead of the two-person comparison.
